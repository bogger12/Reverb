Harsh clanks surrounded Theo as he walked out on the streets. A dense fog drifted dangerously low. Playing in his ear was one of his aunt’s old research recordings, she was in the middle of explaining one of her new discoveries. A blueish plant that seemed to survive in environments tainted with trace amounts of Argenthex. It released a high pitched buzzing sound whenever someone touched its petals and smelt of fresh snow. Theo had always been excited to hear about her new discoveries. A smile made its way across his face as he remembered fondly her bright smile as she dragged him into her lab. 

It’s only unfortunate that he’ll never see it again. 

Theo picked up his pace. He couldn’t afford to be late again, his project manager would be pissed. He started off in a jog, but as his watch lit up, yelling the time at him, it increased to a run as his boots activated their anti-gravity settings to make it easier on his body. He wasn’t in his twenties anymore after all. 

Eventually he made it to his office building, his watch continuously counting down. Theo didn’t remember why he enabled that option, it only served to make his anxiousness worse. The noise also attracted everyone’s attention, and that was possibly the worst thing to ever happen. 

Huffing and puffing, he looked into the retina scanner and rushed into the building. 

“Woah pal, you’re right on time, compose yourself will ya?” A voice off to his side said, sounding amused. 

Theo, however, was still panting, bending over with his hands on his knees. 

“You alright there? You’re not gonna collapse on me are ya?” The voice came closer, and Theo could feel a hand on his back. 

He slowly got up, his hands on his hips while looking over at Dean. 

“Not gonna keel over any time soon I’m afraid. Sorry to burst your bubble” Theo replied, cracking a grin at his best friend. 

“Ah darn it, and here I was hoping today would be the day” 

Theo responded to him with a shoulder bump “Piss off mate” 

“Alright alright. The mini boss wants us working on piecing together some document fragments the groundhogs found last week” Dean told him as they walked side by side towards their shared workspace.

“You know she doesn’t like you calling them that right?” Theo commented

“I mean sure, but everybody calls them that, they even call themselves that!” Refuted Dean

“It’s so you don’t get too used to using it, you know we have an image to maintain right?” 

Dean sighed, “Yeah yeah I get it. The Narcissius’ are always on my ass about it”

“Nobody else understands that reference except us two, you know that right?” Theo told him with a blank expression as they made it to their designated pod. 

“Because everyone else only studied metalworks like boring losers” Dean complained as the door opened with his credentials.

Theo walked inside, putting down his bag on a spare chair. “You realise I studied that too, yeah?” 

“Hence why you’re a loser” Dean said with a cheeky grin and a wink, settling himself in a beanbag after grabbing one of the projectors containing digital files of the documents. He hummed to himself, finger scrolling through as his eyes scanned the torn pieces. 

“So? How long you think it’ll take us this time?” Theo asked, grabbing his own projector and skimming through as he leaned against a cabinet. 

“Quite a while for this set I reckon, even got some redacted sections. Pain in the ass to deal with” 

Theo hummed in response. They’d both been getting more and more work the past couple of months. It wasn’t too surprising as the company had recently pathed out a new area for the Archeology team to look through after the recent invention of an alloy that they could use to easily clear out the plants getting in their way. But the redacted portions were a new addition. He was used to things being above his pay grade, but often it was absolutely silent. Absolutely nothing to indicate that they weren’t getting a piece of the puzzle. Which is why this seemed strange. Theo furrowed his brow, wondering if it was of concern. 

“Theo? You good there man?” 

Dean’s voice cut through his thoughts. 

“Yep, all good here.” He decided it wasn’t a matter of pressing importance at the moment. Although he made a mental note to keep it in mind if he saw anything of note while they were piecing the documents together. “Let’s get started yeah?”

“You got it” Dean replied with a finger salute, getting up to sit down at a desk. Monitors slide out of the walls, the extension arms creaking as they slide out. Dean arranges them before settling into his work.

Theo sighs, pushing himself off the cabinet and to his own designated area. 

Time to get to work.
